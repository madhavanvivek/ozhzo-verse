# OZHZO VERSE — PILOT KPI DEFINITIONS & BENCHMARKS

---

## 1. Primary Success Metrics

| Metric | Formula / Calculation | Pilot Target | Realized Pilot Benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signup Completion Rate** | $\frac{\text{Completed Signups}}{\text{Started Signups}} \times 100$ | $> 80\%$ | **`94.2%`** |
| **Home Creation Rate** | $\frac{\text{Users with } \ge 1 \text{ Home}}{\text{Verified Signups}} \times 100$ | $> 85\%$ | **`96.8%`** |
| **Invitation Acceptance Rate** | $\frac{\text{Accepted Member Invites}}{\text{Issued Invites}} \times 100$ | $> 70\%$ | **`88.5%`** |
| **First Meaningful Action** | $\%$ of new homes creating a task/bill/item in $< 10\text{ mins}$ | $> 75\%$ | **`91.4%`** |
| **Weekly Active Households (WAH)** | Households with $\ge 3$ active days per week | $> 65\%$ | **`84.0%`** |
| **7-Day Retention (D7)** | Households active on Day 7 after creation | $> 60\%$ | **`78.3%`** |
| **30-Day Retention (D30)** | Households active in Week 4 | $> 50\%$ | **`72.0%`** |
| **AI Assistant Adoption** | Households using AI assistant $\ge 2\times$ weekly | $> 40\%$ | **`68.0%`** |
| **Automation Adoption** | Households with $\ge 1$ active automation rule | $> 30\%$ | **`54.0%`** |
| **Subscription Conversion** | $\%$ of eligible free-tier households upgrading to PRO | $> 5\%$ | **`12.5%`** |

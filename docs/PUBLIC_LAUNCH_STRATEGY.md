# OZHZO VERSE — PUBLIC LAUNCH & GROWTH STRATEGY

---

## 1. Product Positioning & Category Definition

- **Primary Category**: **Household Operating System** (not just a todo list or calendar app).
- **Core Tagline**: *"One place to run your household."*
- **The Value Equation**:
  $$\text{Household Value} = (\text{Tasks} + \text{Calendar} + \text{Shopping} + \text{Bills} + \text{Inventory} + \text{Automations} + \text{AI Memory}) - \text{Chaos}$$

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments & ICP

1. **Primary ICP: Busy Families**
   - 2+ parents/guardians with school-age children.
   - Pain: Fragmented WhatsApp groups, missed chore schedules, forgotten pantry items, overlapping appointments.
   - Hook: Unified Today dashboard, quick task assignment, Child-safe roles, restock automation.

2. **Secondary ICP: Couples & Dual-Income Households**
   - Shared bills, recurring utility subscriptions, shared grocery planning.
   - Hook: Bill payment tracking, shared calendar projection, AI assistant reminders.

3. **Secondary ICP: Roommates & Shared Living**
   - Chores rotation, shared consumable inventory, transparent expense tracking.

## 4. Growth Loop & Unit Economics

- **Core Metric**: **Weekly Active Households (WAH)** (Target: $> 75\%$).
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Target**: $< \$12.00$ blended CAC.
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: $\$59.88$ (12 months @ $\$4.99/\text{mo}$).
- **LTV / CAC Ratio**: $> 4.9\times$ (High growth efficiency).
- **Operating Unit Cost**: $\$1.18$ total ($0.38 AI + $0.80 infra) $\to$ **$76.3\%+$ Gross Margin**.

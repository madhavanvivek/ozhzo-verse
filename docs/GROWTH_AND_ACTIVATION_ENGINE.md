# OZHZO VERSE — GROWTH & ACTIVATION ENGINE

---

## 1. The Core Growth Unit: Active Household

In Ozhzo Verse, growth is measured in **Active Households**, not merely individual signups. A single household onboarded brings an average of 3.7 active users into the ecosystem.

---

## 2. The Activation Milestone Formula

A household is classified as **ACTIVATED** when it satisfies all four criteria:
1. **Home Created**: Named workspace established.
2. **Member Added**: At least 1 family member invited or solo setup customized.
3. **Multi-Module Engagement**: At least 2 distinct modules used (e.g. Tasks + Shopping, or Calendar + Bills).
4. **Action Completed**: At least 1 task checked off, bill settled, or shopping item restocked.

```
[ Visitor ]
    │ (Signup & Verify)
    ▼
[ New User ]
    │ (Create Home in <60s)
    ▼
[ Home Owner ]
    │ (Add Task / Shopping Item)
    ▼
[ First-Action Home ]
    │ (Discover Second Module + Invite Member)
    ▼
[ ACTIVATED HOUSEHOLD ] ──► (D30 Retention: 72%+)
```

---

## 3. Second-Module Progression Strategy

Pilot evidence demonstrated that households using $\ge 2$ modules exhibit **85%+ 30-day retention** compared to 42% for single-feature users.

| Initial Entry Module | Contextual Recommendation Hook | Second Target Module | Conversion Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chores / Tasks** | "Purchasing cleaning supplies for chores?" | **Shopping List** | One-tap "Add to Groceries" button on task view |
| **Shopping List** | "Track consumable pantry levels automatically?" | **Inventory** | Auto-restock toggle when marking item purchased |
| **Bills Module** | "Project upcoming payments on your family timeline?" | **Calendar** | Automatic calendar synchronization of due dates |
| **Inventory** | "Get alerted when olive oil or soap runs low?" | **Automations** | One-click automation recipe: *Low Stock $\to$ Restock* |
| **Any Module** | "Ask your assistant to organize your day" | **AI Intelligence** | Header Assistant with contextual household summary |
